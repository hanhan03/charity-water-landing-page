Our Story

Every day, more than 700 million people still live without access to clean water. For many families, that means walking hours just to collect water that isn’t even safe to drink.


charity: water is on a mission to change that — and students around the world are stepping up to help. With something as small as the cost of a coffee, we can fund clean water projects that give hope, health, and opportunity to entire communities.


When students take action together, small sacrifices create big impact. This movement isn’t just about donating — it’s about proving that our generation cares and that we’re ready to make change.
